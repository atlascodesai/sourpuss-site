The character

  • Keep the golden cat, blush background and bold, playful wordmark.
  • Use real app screens as the visual evidence. Never paint replacement app UI into a screenshot.
  • The website ball hangs from the viewport and responds to brushing. Keep the four-toe paw cursor.
  • No decorative emoji, scrolling slogans or invented ratings.
  • Use short, specific captions and generous space. Keep subscription terms visible.
  • Keep raw captures unchanged; compose captions and frames around them.
  • Translate interface, captions and sample content together; do not claim English UI is localized.

Website: Fraunces with DM Sans. Screenshot artwork: Georgia-Bold with AvenirNext-Regular. App typography remains System.

Localization and truth

RC scope: English (en-US) and Spanish (es-ES). English source only today; Spanish translations and captures are pending. Each caption exports a stable key, context and source hash. Two independent reviewer vendors must judge exact translated values; changes invalidate their approval. Translated captions do not establish translated app UI.

Website artwork and App Store artwork derive from the same unmodified raw app captures. The subscription review image remains a separate, unembellished purchase-screen capture. Fixture screenshots prove appearance; real-device tests prove behavior.
