Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data.

How do I export my data?

Go to Profile → Settings → Export Data. You'll receive a file containing all your reviews and account information.

Why can't I see bars near me?

Make sure you've granted location permissions to Sourpuss. Go to your device Settings → Sourpuss → Location and select "While Using the App".

How do I change my username?

Go to Profile → Settings → Edit Profile to update your username.

Can I edit or delete my reviews?

Yes! Tap on any of your reviews to open the detail view, then tap "Edit" to modify or delete it.

How are bar ratings calculated?

Bar ratings are the average of all user reviews for that venue. The more reviews, the more accurate the rating.
