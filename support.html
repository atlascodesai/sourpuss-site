Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?
+

Go to Profile, then tap Settings and select Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data including reviews, favorites, and profile information.

How do I export my data?
+

Navigate to Profile, then Settings and tap Export Data. You'll receive a file containing all your reviews, favorites, and account information in a portable format.

Why can't I see bars near me?
+

Make sure you've granted location permissions to Sourpuss. Go to your device's Settings app, find Sourpuss, tap Location, and select "While Using the App" to enable nearby bar discovery.

How do I change my username?
+

Head to Profile, then Settings, and tap Edit Profile. From there you can update your username and other profile details.

Can I edit or delete my reviews?
+

Absolutely! Tap on any of your reviews to open the detail view, then tap the Edit button to modify your rating, notes, or photos. You can also delete the review entirely from this screen.

How are bar ratings calculated?
+

Bar ratings are the average of all user reviews for that venue. The more reviews a bar receives, the more accurate and representative its rating becomes. Your reviews help the whole community!
